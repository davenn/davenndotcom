Definitions
Time in range
Share of readings from 70 to 180 mg/dL. Below range splits into 54–69 (level 1) and under 54 (level 2); above range into 181–250 and over 250.
GMI
Glucose management indicator, an estimate of A1c from mean CGM glucose: 3.31 + 0.02392 × mean mg/dL. It can differ from a lab A1c.
CV
Coefficient of variation: standard deviation divided by the mean. At or under 36% is considered stable.
Time in tight range
Share of readings from 70 to 140 mg/dL. Over 50% has been proposed as a goal; it is not yet a consensus target.
Glycemia Risk Index
A single 0–100 score weighted toward very low and very high readings (Klonoff et al., 2023): 3.0 × under 54 + 2.4 × 54–69 + 1.6 × over 250 + 0.8 × 181–250. Zones A to E are bands of 20 points, A the lowest risk.
Lows and highs
Episodes start after 15 minutes past the threshold and end after 15 minutes back across it. A gap of more than 15 minutes in the readings ends an episode.
Before and after a low
The comparison figures take the same 3-hour-before and 2-hour-after windows at every 30 minutes across the whole period.
