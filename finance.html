Unlock

Enter your passphrase. It never leaves this device. Two keys are made from it here: one the server checks, and one that encrypts your numbers.

First-time setup

The server has no Finance key yet. Type the same passphrase again to confirm it. Use at least 12 characters, and keep it somewhere safe: there is no reset. Without it nobody can read the saved data, including the server.

Add this line to .env beside api.php on the host, then press Unlock.
